How to Get the TeraBox Cookie

Since TeraBox is highly restricted, some files require browser authentication. Follow these simple steps to retrieve your ndus cookie.

01

Log in to TeraBox

Open a new browser tab, go to terabox.com, and log in to your account.

02

Open Developer Tools

Press F12 (or right-click anywhere and select Inspect) to open Developer Tools, then navigate to the Application tab (Chrome) or Storage tab (Firefox).

03

Copy the ndus Cookie

Under Cookies, select https://www.terabox.com, search for the cookie named ndus, and copy its value.

04

Save in Settings

Paste the copied cookie value in the Advanced Options panel or in the Cookie Settings tab to persist it.
